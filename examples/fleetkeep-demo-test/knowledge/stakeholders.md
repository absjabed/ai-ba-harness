# Stakeholders

Who is involved, what they own, and what they can decide. The harness uses the **Can decide** column
to tell a decision from an opinion (conventions, rule 5). Use roles; add a name only where people
need to know who to go to.

| Role | Name (optional) | Organisation | Interest | Owns | Can decide | Contact route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product owner | | | What the product does and what each release delivers | Product behaviour, business rules, scope, release content | Product behaviour, business rules, scope and release content | |
| Lead BA | | | A knowledge base and requirements the team can trust | The knowledge base; requirement wording | What enters the knowledge base, and requirement wording. Not product behaviour | |
| Engineering lead | | | How the product is built, and what it costs | Technical approach; estimates | Technical approach and estimates. Not business rules | |
| Compliance adviser | | | Keeping the product in line with the regulator's guidance | Interpretation of regulator guidance | How regulator guidance is interpreted. Scope stays with the Product owner | |
| Pilot transport manager | | Pilot customer | Running a road-legal fleet with the product | | Nothing in the product | |
| Pilot workshop supervisor | | Pilot customer | Running the workshop with the product | | Nothing in the product | |

## RACI for recurring decisions

| Decision | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A change to a business rule | Lead BA | Product owner | Compliance adviser, Engineering lead | Pilot transport manager |
| Release content | Product owner | Product owner | Engineering lead, Lead BA | Support |
| Interpreting regulator guidance | Compliance adviser | Product owner | Lead BA | |
| Accepting a knowledge base change | Lead BA | Lead BA | BA | |
