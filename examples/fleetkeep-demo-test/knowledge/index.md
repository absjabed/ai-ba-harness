## Decisions

- [DEC-001 Next due date counts from the anchor date](decisions/DEC-001-due-date-counts-from-the-anchor-date.md) · approved · The next safety inspection due date is calculated from the vehicle's anchor date and interval, not from the date the last inspection was done; a late inspection doesn't move the schedule, and an early inspection doesn't move it either.
- [DEC-002 Inspection due banding on the dashboard](decisions/DEC-002-inspection-due-banding.md) · approved · The dashboard shows a vehicle's safety inspection as green when it is due in more than 14 days, amber at 14 days or less, and red when overdue.
- [DEC-003 Dashboard load time for large fleets](decisions/DEC-003-dashboard-load-time-for-large-fleets.md) · approved · The dashboard loads in under 3 seconds for fleets of up to 2,000 vehicles.
