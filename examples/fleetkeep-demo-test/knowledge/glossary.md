## Anchor date
**T-001** · status: confirmed · ([SRC-001](sources/SRC-001-2026-10-14-inspections-workshop.md))

The date a vehicle's safety inspection schedule counts from; the next due date is calculated from it.

**Also called:** start date (used by the pilot customer; avoid, see PROJECT-PROFILE.md section 7)
**See:** [CAP-002 Calculating the next safety inspection due date](domains/inspections/CAP-002-calculating-the-next-inspection-due-date.md)

## Inspection due status
**T-002** · status: confirmed · ([SRC-002](sources/SRC-002-2026-10-15-requirements-due-dates-and-banding.md))

The band the dashboard shows for a vehicle's next safety inspection: green, amber or red (BR-003).

**Also called:** RAG banding
**See:** [CAP-003 Seeing inspection due status on the dashboard](domains/inspections/CAP-003-seeing-inspection-due-status-on-the-dashboard.md)
