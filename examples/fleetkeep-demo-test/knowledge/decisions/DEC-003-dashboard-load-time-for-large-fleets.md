# Dashboard load time for large fleets

The dashboard loads in under 3 seconds for fleets of up to 2,000 vehicles.

## Context

Answers [Q-003](../questions/Q-003-dashboard-load-time-for-large-fleets.md), raised because the
workshop notes said only that the dashboard "needs to load fast for big fleets". The Product owner
agreed the target by email on 2026-10-15; the Lead BA relayed it while writing the inspection due
dates and banding requirement
([SRC-002](../sources/SRC-002-2026-10-15-requirements-due-dates-and-banding.md)). The email itself
isn't kept in this repository.

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|

## Decision

Under 3 seconds for fleets of up to 2,000 vehicles. No reason was recorded.

- Answers Q-003; recorded on [CAP-003 Seeing inspection due status on the dashboard](../domains/inspections/CAP-003-seeing-inspection-due-status-on-the-dashboard.md).
