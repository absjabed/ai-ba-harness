# Inspection due banding on the dashboard

The dashboard shows a vehicle's safety inspection as green when it is due in more than 14 days,
amber at 14 days or less, and red when overdue.

## Context

Agreed by the Product owner at the inspections workshop of 2026-10-14 ("agreed, ship it like that")
([SRC-001](../sources/SRC-001-2026-10-14-inspections-workshop.md)).

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|

## Decision

Green when due in more than 14 days, amber at 14 days or less, red when overdue. No reason was
recorded.

## Consequences

- BR-003 on [CAP-003 Seeing inspection due status on the dashboard](../domains/inspections/CAP-003-seeing-inspection-due-status-on-the-dashboard.md).
