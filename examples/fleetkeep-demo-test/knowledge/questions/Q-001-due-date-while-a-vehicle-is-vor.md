# What happens to the due date while a vehicle is VOR?

What happens to a vehicle's next safety inspection due date while the vehicle is VOR, especially for
a long time?

## Where it comes up

- [CAP-002 Calculating the next safety inspection due date](../domains/inspections/CAP-002-calculating-the-next-inspection-due-date.md)
  (BR-002).

## What is unclear

The Engineering lead asked "what happens to the due date when a vehicle is VOR for a long time".
Nobody knew; the Product owner is to think about it
([SRC-001](../sources/SRC-001-2026-10-14-inspections-workshop.md)). "A long time" has no measure.
The due date was set for 2026-10-28 by the Lead BA; no date was given in the meeting.

## Possible answers

1. The schedule keeps running, and the vehicle is overdue when it returns.
2. The schedule pauses while the vehicle is VOR.
3. An inspection is required before the vehicle returns to service.

## Why it matters

The due date rule (BR-002) can't be completed, built or tested for VOR vehicles until this is
answered.
