# Should closing an inspection be blocked without a brake test result?

Should FleetKeep block closing a safety inspection that has no brake performance test result?

## Where it comes up

- [CAP-004 Recording a safety inspection](../domains/inspections/CAP-004-recording-a-safety-inspection.md)
  (BR-004).

## What is unclear

The Compliance adviser said a brake performance test is needed at every safety inspection. Asked
whether closing an inspection without a brake test result should be blocked, the Product owner said
"probably yes, but let me check with the pilot"
([SRC-001](../sources/SRC-001-2026-10-14-inspections-workshop.md)). That is a leaning, not a
decision. The due date was set for 2026-10-28 by the Lead BA; no date was given in the meeting.

## Possible answers

1. Yes: an inspection can't be closed until a brake test result is recorded (the Product owner's
   leaning).
2. No: it can be closed without one.

## Why it matters

What happens when an inspection is closed without a brake test result can't be specified, built or
tested until this is decided.
