# Product owner's inspection rules, draft v2

The Product owner's seven numbered rules for vehicle safety inspections, used to seed the knowledge base.

## Summary

Seven rules: each vehicle has its own inspection interval in whole weeks; the schedule counts from an anchor date and doesn't move when an inspection is early or late; the dashboard bands vehicles green (more than 14 days to the next due date), amber (14 days or less) and red (overdue); an inspection up to 7 days late is not counted as overdue; every inspection includes a brake performance test with its result recorded; only a transport manager sets or changes the interval and anchor date; and the treatment of VOR vehicles is still to be decided. The document is a draft, so its rules are recorded as `confirmed`, not `approved`. The grace period disagrees with the Compliance adviser's regulator guidance summary (5 Oct 2026), so it was recorded as a question, not a rule.
