## Summary

The operator must decide and write down a safety inspection interval for each vehicle. An inspection carried out after its due date is late; the guidance has no grace period, and late inspections must be visible to an auditor. Each safety inspection must include a brake performance test, with the result kept. Inspection records must be kept for at least 15 months. The summary ends with a contact for questions, whose personal details are not recorded here. It agrees with the Product owner's rules (SRC-001) on the per-vehicle interval and the brake test, and disagrees with them on the grace period, which is open as Q-002.
