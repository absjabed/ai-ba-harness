## Summary

The Compliance adviser says a brake test result needs a pass or fail, and for a failed test the reading; the email doesn't say whether this comes from the regulator guidance. They ask whether an inspection can still be closed when its brake test fails, give their view (no), and say it is the Product owner's call. They name a risk that depots without a roller brake tester can't record a reading at all. The email gives no names or contact details.
