# Can a defect be re-categorised after it is reported

Can a defect's category be changed after the driver has reported it, and if so, by whom?

## Where it comes up

- [Reporting a defect in a walkaround check](../domains/defects/CAP-005-reporting-a-defect-in-a-walkaround-check.md).

## What is unclear

At the defects workshop "Nobody knew whether a defect can be re-categorised after it's reported", and the Product owner is to decide ([SRC-003](../sources/SRC-003-2026-10-16-defects-workshop.md), bullet 4).

## Possible answers

1. No: the category the driver gives stays.
2. Yes, by a named role (for example the transport manager when deciding what happens to the defect), with the change recorded.
3. Something else the Product owner decides.

## Why it matters

Whether a vehicle stays VOR when a category-A defect is re-categorised, and what the defect record keeps, depend on the answer.
