# How an auditor sees late inspections

Where and how does the product make late inspections visible to an auditor?

## Where it comes up

- [Recording a safety inspection](../domains/inspections/CAP-004-recording-a-safety-inspection.md).
- [Q-002](Q-002-grace-period-before-an-inspection-is-overdue.md) decides which inspections count as late or overdue.

## What is unclear

The regulator guidance summary says "late inspections must be visible to an auditor" ([SRC-002](../sources/SRC-002-2026-10-05-regulator-inspection-guidance.md), bullet 2), but not what the auditor needs to see, or whether the auditor uses the product or receives something from the operator.

## Possible answers

1. Each inspection record shows whether it was done after its due date, and by how many days.
2. A report of late inspections for a chosen period, which the operator gives the auditor.
3. Both.

## Why it matters

Recording an inspection, and any audit report, can't be specified until it is known what has to be shown and to whom. What the guidance requires the auditor to see is for the Compliance adviser to confirm; how the product shows it is for the Product owner.
