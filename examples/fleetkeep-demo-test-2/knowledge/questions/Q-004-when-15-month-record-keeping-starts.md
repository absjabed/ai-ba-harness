# When the 15-month record keeping starts and who keeps the records

From which date are inspection records kept for at least 15 months, and does the guidance expect the product to keep them, or the operator?

## Where it comes up

- [Recording a safety inspection](../domains/inspections/CAP-004-recording-a-safety-inspection.md).

## What is unclear

The regulator guidance summary says "Inspection records must be kept for at least 15 months." ([SRC-002](../sources/SRC-002-2026-10-05-regulator-inspection-guidance.md), bullet 4), but not the date the period counts from, or whether keeping them falls to the product or to the operator by other means.

## Possible answers

1. 15 months from the inspection date, with the records kept in the product.
2. 15 months from the inspection date, kept by the operator; the product only has to let them take the records out.

## Why it matters

How long the product keeps inspection records, and whether it may ever remove them, can't be specified until this is known. Whether the product itself keeps them is then for the Product owner to decide.
