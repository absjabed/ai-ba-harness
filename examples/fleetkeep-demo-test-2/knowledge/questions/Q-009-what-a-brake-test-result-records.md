# What a brake test result records

What does the product record as the result of a safety inspection's brake performance test: a pass or fail, a reading, or both, and when is the reading needed?

## Where it comes up

- [Recording a safety inspection](../domains/inspections/CAP-004-recording-a-safety-inspection.md), rule BR-005.

## What is unclear

BR-005 says every safety inspection includes a brake performance test "and its result is recorded" ([SRC-001](../sources/SRC-001-2026-10-02-product-owner-inspection-rules.md)), without saying what the result is. The Compliance adviser wrote: "The brake test result needs a pass or fail, and for a fail the reading." ([SRC-004](../sources/SRC-004-2026-10-17-brake-test-results.md), paragraph 1). The email doesn't say whether this comes from the regulator guidance, which the Compliance adviser interprets, or is their suggestion for the product; what the product records is the Product owner's decision. It also doesn't say what the reading is a reading of, or in what unit.

## Possible answers

1. As the Compliance adviser proposes: every brake test records a pass or fail, and a failed test also records the reading.
2. Every brake test records a pass or fail and the reading, whether it passed or failed.

## Why it matters

The inputs for recording a safety inspection can't be specified until this is known. If a reading is required, depots without a roller brake tester may not be able to record one (R-002 in [the RAID log](../raid.md)). Whether the regulator guidance requires the reading is for the Compliance adviser to confirm; what the product records is for the Product owner.
