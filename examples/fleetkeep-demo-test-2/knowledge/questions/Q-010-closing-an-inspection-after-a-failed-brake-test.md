# Can an inspection be closed when its brake test fails

If a safety inspection's brake performance test fails, can the inspection still be closed, and what does closing an inspection mean?

## Where it comes up

- [Recording a safety inspection](../domains/inspections/CAP-004-recording-a-safety-inspection.md), which has no failure paths yet.

## What is unclear

The Compliance adviser asked: "If the brake test fails, can the inspection still be closed? I'd say no, but that's the Product owner's call." ([SRC-004](../sources/SRC-004-2026-10-17-brake-test-results.md), paragraph 1). Their view is no; the decision is the Product owner's. The knowledge base doesn't yet say what closing an inspection means or when it happens.

## Possible answers

1. No, as the Compliance adviser suggests: an inspection whose brake test failed stays open. What then lets it close is still to be decided.
2. Yes: the inspection is closed with the failed result recorded. What follows for the vehicle is still to be decided.

## Why it matters

What happens when a brake test fails, and so the failure paths of recording a safety inspection, can't be specified until this is answered.
