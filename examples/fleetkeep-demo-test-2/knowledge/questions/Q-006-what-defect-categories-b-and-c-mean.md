# What defect categories B and C mean

What makes a defect category B or C, and what follows from each for the vehicle and the defect?

## Where it comes up

- [Reporting a defect in a walkaround check](../domains/defects/CAP-005-reporting-a-defect-in-a-walkaround-check.md).

## What is unclear

The defects workshop notes say drivers report defects "each with a category A, B or C" ([SRC-003](../sources/SRC-003-2026-10-16-defects-workshop.md), bullet 1). Only category A was given a consequence at the workshop: it takes the vehicle off the road immediately (bullet 2). Nothing says what makes a defect category B or C, or what happens because of it.

## Possible answers

1. B and C are less serious than A: the vehicle stays in use, and each category has its own rule for when the defect must be dealt with.
2. Something else the Product owner decides.

## Why it matters

Reporting a defect and deciding what happens to it can't be specified for category-B and category-C defects until the categories are defined.
