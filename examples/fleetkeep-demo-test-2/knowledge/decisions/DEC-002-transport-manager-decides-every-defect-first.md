# Every defect gets a decision from the transport manager first

Every reported defect gets a decision from the transport manager first.

## Context

At the defects workshop on 2026-10-16 the Pilot workshop supervisor said technicians sometimes clear category-C defects without a job card ([SRC-003](../sources/SRC-003-2026-10-16-defects-workshop.md), bullet 3). The Product owner made this decision in the same discussion.

## Options considered

Not recorded in the source.

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|

## Decision

The Product owner decided: "Every defect gets a decision from the transport manager first." No reason was recorded. Which decisions the transport manager can make, and what the decision comes before, is [Q-007](../questions/Q-007-transport-manager-decision-on-a-defect.md).

## Consequences

- [Deciding what happens to a reported defect](../domains/defects/CAP-006-deciding-what-happens-to-a-reported-defect.md) gets rule BR-007.
- The [Transport manager](../entities/ENT-001-transport-manager.md) role gains this permission.
