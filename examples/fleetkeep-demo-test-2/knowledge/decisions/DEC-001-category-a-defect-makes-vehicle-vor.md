# A category-A defect makes the vehicle VOR immediately

A defect reported with category A takes the vehicle off the road (VOR) immediately.

## Context

At the defects workshop on 2026-10-16 the attendees went through how drivers report defects in the walkaround check, each with a category A, B or C ([SRC-003](../sources/SRC-003-2026-10-16-defects-workshop.md)). What makes a vehicle VOR was an open question ([Q-003](../questions/Q-003-inspection-rules-for-vor-vehicles.md)).

## Options considered

Not recorded in the source.

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|

## Decision

The Product owner decided that a category-A defect takes the vehicle off the road (VOR) immediately. No reason was recorded.

## Consequences

- [Reporting a defect in a walkaround check](../domains/defects/CAP-005-reporting-a-defect-in-a-walkaround-check.md) gets rule BR-006.
- [Q-003](../questions/Q-003-inspection-rules-for-vor-vehicles.md) stays open: this is one thing that makes a vehicle VOR, but whether anything else does, and how the inspection rules apply while a vehicle is VOR, isn't decided.
