# Regulator guidance on safety inspections: summary for FleetKeep (Compliance adviser, 5 Oct 2026)

- The operator must decide and write down a safety inspection interval for each vehicle.
- An inspection carried out after its due date is late. There is no grace period in the guidance;
  late inspections must be visible to an auditor.
- Each safety inspection must include a brake performance test, with the result kept.
- Inspection records must be kept for at least 15 months.
