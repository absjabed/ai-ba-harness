# Regulator roadworthiness guidance, summary for FleetKeep

The regulator's public guidance on keeping vehicles roadworthy, as summarised by the Compliance
adviser, which set out what FleetKeep's records must show an auditor.

## Summary

As summarised by the Compliance adviser: drivers are expected to check a vehicle before using it and
to report defects in writing; a defect that affects roadworthiness has to be put right before the
vehicle is used; and operators keep their maintenance records, including checks, defects and repairs,
for at least 15 months, so they can show an auditor a vehicle's full history. The covering note said
FleetKeep's walkaround check and audit trail are the operator's evidence for all three, and asked
what happens when a driver has no signal at the depot.

This summary records only what the knowledge base uses. The guidance itself is the authority; read
it, not this record, for anything regulatory.
