## Capabilities

### defects

- [CAP-001 Reporting a defect from a walkaround check](domains/defects/CAP-001-reporting-a-defect-from-a-walkaround-check.md) · confirmed · A driver records any defect found during the walkaround check of an assigned vehicle, so the fault is on record and, if it is immobilising, the vehicle is taken off the road at once.
- [CAP-002 Triaging a defect](domains/defects/CAP-002-triaging-a-defect.md) · confirmed · The transport manager looks at each reported defect and either raises a job card for it or rejects it with a reason, so every defect gets a decision and none is lost.
- [CAP-003 Escalating an unactioned category-A defect](domains/defects/CAP-003-escalating-an-unactioned-category-a-defect.md) · superseded · FleetKeep alerts the transport manager when a category-A defect has waited too long for triage, so an immobilised vehicle isn't forgotten.
- [CAP-004 Escalating unactioned defects](domains/defects/CAP-004-escalating-unactioned-defects.md) · confirmed · FleetKeep alerts the transport manager when a category-A or category-B defect has waited longer than its category allows without a triage outcome, so no serious defect sits unnoticed.

### vehicles

- [CAP-006 Authorising return to service](domains/vehicles/CAP-006-authorising-return-to-service.md) · approved · A transport manager authorises a vehicle that was off the road (VOR) to be driven again, once it is safe and legal to use.

### workshop

- [CAP-005 Closing a job card](domains/workshop/CAP-005-closing-a-job-card.md) · draft · A workshop technician closes a job card once the work on the vehicle is done, recording labour, parts and the outcome of each defect, so the repair is on record and depot stock stays accurate.

- [DEC-001 Escalation clock starts when the defect is reported](decisions/DEC-001-escalation-clock-starts-at-report.md) · approved · The time a defect may wait before it escalates is counted from the moment the driver reports it, not from triage or the start of the next shift.
- [DEC-002 Escalated defects are flagged at the top of the workshop queue](decisions/DEC-002-escalated-defects-top-of-workshop-queue.md) · confirmed · When a defect escalates, it is flagged at the top of the workshop queue of the vehicle's depot, as well as being sent to the transport manager.
- [DEC-003 Return to service stays with the transport manager, with no override](decisions/DEC-003-return-to-service-conditions.md) · approved · Only a transport manager can return a VOR vehicle to service, only once every category-A defect on it is closed and no inspection is overdue, and there is no override.
